05 / FEATURED Computer Vision

Citrus Detection & Ripeness Classification

My flagship project: a multi-stage pipeline that finds citrus fruit in dense orchard video, filters out leaves that get mistaken for unripe fruit, then classifies what's left as ripe or unripe. Rebuilt several times as real footage kept breaking assumptions the training data made.

  • YOLO11m fruit detector tuned for small, distant fruit (imgsz 1280) on merged Roboflow datasets, trained on an RTX A5000
  • Dedicated single-class leaf detector to suppress false positives from dense foliage read as unripe fruit
  • Ripeness call moved from a hand-tuned HSV heuristic to a trained YOLO classifier for more reliable results
  • Iterated through multiple dataset sources and self-labeled video frames to close the training-to-real-footage gap
YOLO11 PyTorch OpenCV Agritech Computer Vision

Annoying Alarm
